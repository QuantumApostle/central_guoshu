Nanjing · 1928

A school for the national art

The Central Guoshu Institute gathered Chinese martial arts into one public school: a place to name the methods, train teachers, and put private practice into civic life.

  • 1928 Founded in Nanjing under the Republic of China.
  • 国术 Guoshu, “national art,” the name given to Chinese martial arts.
  • One roof Many lineages. The institute taught them; it did not invent them.

The institute

Zhang Zhijiang led the school. Its job was to turn scattered teaching into a curriculum that armies, schools, and public examinations could use.

Read the history

The practice

Students learned empty-hand forms, weapons, paired work, and the public sparring of the leitai. Internal and external methods sat in two departments.
